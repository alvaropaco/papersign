Certifications and regulatory frameworks define different security, privacy, and operational requirements. This page explains their scope and common requirements; it does not represent a certification or compliance determination for Papersign or any deployment.

## 21 CFR Part 11

21 CFR Part 11 is a regulation by the FDA that establishes the criteria for electronic records and electronic signatures to ensure their authenticity, integrity, and confidentiality in the pharmaceutical, medical device, and other FDA-regulated industries.

### Main Requirements

- Strong identity checks for each signature
- Signature and audit trails
- User access management
- Quality assurance documentation

## SOC 2

SOC 2 is a framework for managing and auditing security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, and data privacy in cloud and IT service organizations, established by the American Institute of Certified Public Accountants (AICPA).

SOC 2 reports are based on an independent audit of an organization's controls against selected Trust Services Criteria. The scope and period covered by a report determine what the report evaluates.

## ISO 27001

ISO 27001 is an international standard for managing information security, specifying requirements for establishing, implementing, maintaining, and continually improving an information security management system (ISMS).

Certification involves an independent assessment of an organization's ISMS against the standard's requirements within a defined scope.

## HIPAA

The Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) is a U.S. law that establishes privacy and security requirements for protected health information. Covered entities and business associates must assess their obligations and implement appropriate administrative, physical, and technical safeguards for their use case.
